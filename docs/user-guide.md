# User guide

This guide is for **employees**, **managers** and **calibration owners**. Administrators see the
[Administrator guide](admin-guide.md).

## Signing in

Your administrator sends you the app link, your work email and a temporary password. At first sign-in
you must choose your own password (minimum 10 characters). You can change it any time via **Password** in the top bar.

## For employees: the self-assessment

The assessment takes roughly 20–30 minutes. You can save a draft and continue later.

### 1. Confirm your role
Open **My assessment**. The app shows your role (e.g. *CRM Campaign Manager*). The areas you are asked
about depend on it.
- **Yes, this is my role** → the assessment starts.
- **No, my role is different** → describe the correct role. The administrator is notified and fixes it
  before you start.

### 2. Rate your current level
You only see areas **your role requires** (required level 1–4). They are grouped into
**Knowledge**, **Skills** and **Competencies**. Each area shows:
- the **definition** and **what it looks like** (observable statement),
- five level cards **0–4** with a description that fits the type (Knowledge / Skill / Competency),
- an example of typical evidence.

Rate what you **do today**, not what you think your role expects. The required level is deliberately
hidden until you submit, so it does not influence your answer.

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | No knowledge or experience in this area yet |
| 1 Basic | Basic awareness / simple tasks with guidance |
| 2 Working | Independent in regular day-to-day work |
| 3 Advanced | Complex situations, improves practices, guides others |
| 4 Expert | Recognised expertise, sets standards and direction. Not just "a very good Copilot user". |

### 3. Add evidence
- **Required for levels 2–4**, recommended for level 1.
- What counts as evidence depends on the type:
  - **Knowledge**: explanation of a concept, knowledge check, relevant completed course, recognising risks.
  - **Skill**: an actual output: a prompt, an AI-assisted analysis, a validated output, an automation.
  - **Competency**: application in your work: a delivered use case, process change, measured benefit, controls introduced.
- A course completion supports *Knowledge* but does not prove a *Skill* or *Competency*.
- Rule of thumb: L1 a simple example · L2 at least one work example · L3 several examples or a complex output ·
  L4 evidence of setting standards, leading others or organisational impact.
- **Do not paste confidential, customer or personal data.** Put a link to an approved location
  (SharePoint, Teams…) in *Evidence link* instead.
- Select your **confidence** (Low / Medium / High). It helps your manager focus the conversation and does not change the score.

### 4. Submit
Tick the confirmation and click **Submit to manager**. If anything is missing, the affected areas are
highlighted. After submission the assessment is **locked**. Your manager can return it to you if changes are needed.

### 5. Results
- **Before validation** you see an *indicative* result: your **Self Gap** = Required − Self (never below 0).
- **After your manager finalises**, you see the **official Development Gap** based on the validated level,
  your **development priorities** (sorted by gap × required level), a breakdown by Knowledge / Skill / Competency,
  and a flag if there is a gap in a **critical control area** (e.g. security & privacy, output validation).

If your role's requirements have not yet been validated by management, a yellow banner says so. Gaps may still change.

## For managers: review and validation

**Team reviews** lists your direct reports with their status for the selected cycle.

### Manager review (status *Submitted* / *Manager Review*)
Open an assessment with **Review**. For every area you see the required level, the self level, confidence and evidence.
1. Select your **manager level** (0–4) based on observable performance and the evidence.
2. If your level **differs** from the self level, a **comment is mandatory**. It explains the difference and is a useful
   start for the development conversation.
3. **Save review** at any time; **Complete review → calibration** when all areas are rated.

Do not average the two ratings. A level such as 2.5 has no meaning on the behavioural scale.

### Calibration (status *Calibration*)
Set the **validated current level** for each area: a whole number agreed after review/calibration (for example
in a short calibration conversation or team calibration session). It becomes the official current level.
- **Pre-fill empty with manager levels** copies your manager level into empty fields as a starting point. Check before finalising.
- Set the **next review date** (default: 12 months).
- **Finalise assessment** publishes the official gap to the employee and to reports.

### Returning / reopening
Under **Return to employee / reopen** you can send the assessment back to *Draft* (with a reason). The version number
increases and the action is logged. A finalised result is withdrawn until it is finalised again.

### Reports
**Gap reports** shows, for the people in your scope, the Team Gap Summary (by area, role, role family, type),
the most common gaps, and the Employee Gap Summary. **Export to Excel** downloads all records plus summaries.
Only finalised assessments count in official figures.

## For calibration owners

A calibration owner (e.g. from People & Culture or L&D) can see all assessments, reports and exports of their team and
can set validated levels, finalise and reopen. Manager review itself remains with the line manager.

## Workflow at a glance

```
Draft ──submit──▶ Submitted ──manager saves──▶ Manager Review ──complete──▶ Calibration ──finalise──▶ Finalized
  ▲                                                                                                    │
  └─────────────────────────────── reopen (version + 1) ◀─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
