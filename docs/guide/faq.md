# FAQ and glossary

## Frequently asked questions

**I can't sign in.**
Check that you use your work email. If you forgot your password, ask your administrator for a new temporary password.

**My role is wrong.**
On the start page choose *No, my role is different* and describe your correct role. The administrator will fix it.
If you have already started, tell your manager or administrator.

**Why can't I see the required level while rating myself?**
So that it doesn't influence your rating. You see it right after you submit.

**Do I have to fill in every area?**
Only the areas your role requires are shown, and all of them must be rated before you can submit.

**Is level 0 a bad answer?**
No. It is an honest starting point and helps target learning.

**I submitted by mistake / want to change an answer.**
Ask your manager to return the assessment to you. It becomes a Draft again.

**Why is my result "indicative"?**
Until your manager finalises the assessment, the app compares your self-rating with the requirement. The official
result uses the validated level.

**My manager rated me lower. What now?**
Read the comment, discuss the evidence together, and agree on development actions. The validated level is agreed after
that conversation; it is not an average.

**Is this a performance rating?**
No. It shows development needs against the role's AI requirements. The priority score is only for ordering learning priorities.

**Who can see my assessment?**
You, your manager, the calibration owner of your team and administrators.

**The app looks different from the screenshots.**
Screenshots use demo data, so names and areas differ. If a button or step is different, tell the administrator so the guide can be updated.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Area** | One item of the framework, e.g. *S01 Prompting*. Each has a code: K = Knowledge, S = Skill, C = Competency. |
| **Required level** | The level (0–4) the role needs. Set per role and validated with management. |
| **Self level** | Your own rating of your current level. |
| **Manager level** | Your manager's evidence-based rating. |
| **Validated (current) level** | The final agreed level used for the official result. |
| **Self Gap** | Required − self level (never below 0). Indicative only. |
| **Development gap** | Required − validated level (never below 0). The official gap. |
| **Surplus** | Validated − required level, if above the requirement. Never offsets gaps elsewhere. |
| **Priority score** | Development gap × required level (0–16), used to order development priorities. |
| **Critical control area (CC)** | An area where any gap is flagged, e.g. data protection or checking AI output. |
| **Cycle** | An assessment period, e.g. *2027 Annual*. |
| **Calibration** | Agreeing validated levels consistently across a team. |
| **Finalised** | The assessment is complete and the official result is published. |
| **Role validation** | Management confirmation that a role's required levels are correct. |
